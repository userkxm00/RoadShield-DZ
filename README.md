# RoadShield DZ

**AI-Powered Road Safety & Risk Intelligence Platform**

RoadShield DZ is an innovation project focused on improving road safety in Algeria by turning mobile and vehicle telemetry into structured, location-aware road-risk intelligence.

## Mission

Detect and aggregate road hazards and abnormal road-surface signals, build a live road-risk map, and provide useful safety intelligence to drivers, fleets, and authorized road-safety stakeholders.

## Core MVP thesis

RoadShield does **not** attempt to replace navigation products or official crash-reporting systems. It adds a preventative, field-sourced intelligence layer based on:

- GPS
- smartphone motion sensors
- optional camera signals
- repeated observations from multiple trips
- geospatial aggregation
- transparent risk scoring

## Guiding principles

1. Safety before feature count.
2. Evidence before claims.
3. Privacy by design.
4. No paid AI/API dependency in the MVP core.
5. Every feature must be testable.
6. Every major architectural decision must be documented.
7. Do not claim that RoadShield can guarantee accident prevention.
8. Build the smallest demonstrable system first, then expand.
9. Codex is the implementation agent; product and architecture decisions require human approval.
10. A gate is not passed by documentation alone; acceptance evidence must exist.
