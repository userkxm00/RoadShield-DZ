# RoadShield DZ — Competition & Label Plan

## Target
Best Innovative Project — President of the Republic Prize, 2026 edition.

## Official facts checked 25 August 2026
- The official competition platform states that candidature is reserved for holders of a valid official label: Projet Innovant, Startup, or Scale-up.
- For the 2026 edition, the official platform states the application deadline is **10 September 2026**.
- The official platform states the Best Innovative Project award is **DZD 3,000,000**.
- The platform states that the product/service/project must be an innovative solution linked to new technologies.
- A Projet Innovant holder who has already obtained Startup must compete in the Startup category instead; categories are tied to the label status.

## Current label status
RoadShield has no label in this repository. Obtaining a valid Projet Innovant label is a critical external dependency for candidature.

## Label application evidence to prepare
Startup.dz's current Projet Innovant form includes:
- applicant identity and founder information;
- co-founder information and CVs;
- project name and sector;
- short description of the project and why it is innovative (400-character limit);
- Innovation-focused Pitch Deck;
- detailed project presentation and innovation aspects;
- prototype photo;
- project progress level;
- business model type (B2B/B2C/B2G/other);
- website;
- incubation details;
- intellectual-property information, if any;
- competitions/rewards, if any.

## Competition dossier strategy
The final package should communicate one coherent story:
1. A major Algerian road-safety problem.
2. Evidence that the problem is real.
3. A precise gap not already solved by existing systems.
4. The RoadShield innovation.
5. A working prototype.
6. Controlled validation results.
7. Market and stakeholder model.
8. Privacy and safety-by-design.
9. A credible Algeria-first scale path.

## Required proof before submission
- live or recorded MVP demonstration;
- prototype images;
- architecture diagram;
- test methodology;
- measured results with sample size and limitations;
- competitor comparison;
- clear founder/team roles;
- website/landing page;
- consistent project naming across every document.

## Risk: timing
The deadline is close. The repository must prioritize a narrow, credible prototype over breadth. The label application itself is an external process and must not be represented as guaranteed until an official valid label is actually issued.

## Claims policy
Do not claim the project has won, has received the label, or has measured accident reduction unless an official document or RoadShield experiment establishes it.
