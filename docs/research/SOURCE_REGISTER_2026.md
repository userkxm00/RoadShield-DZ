## Claiming rules

- Official national statistics must cite the primary government/APS source.
- Competitor capabilities must cite current product documentation.
- Technical claims must cite a paper or a RoadShield measurement.
- RoadShield performance metrics may not appear publicly until measured and reproduced.
- Hypotheses must be labelled as hypotheses.
- Targets must be labelled as targets.
- Never claim “first”, “unique”, “proven”, “patented”, or “AI-powered” as a novelty assertion without evidence.

## Current research conclusion

The evidence supports the seriousness of road safety and the active Algerian road-maintenance/safety ecosystem. It does not yet prove that RoadShield’s specific repeated-observation evidence workflow is superior to existing alternatives. That remains an explicit validation objective.
