# RoadShield DZ — G1.3 Interview Form v1

## Intro

We are conducting short research interviews about road-condition information and road safety. We are not testing a product and there are no right answers. We want to understand how people currently handle road hazards.

Do not record names or other unnecessary personal identifiers in the research log.

## Participant profile

- Segment: Regular driver / Professional driver / Fleet operator / Road or maintenance stakeholder / Other
- Approximate experience: <1 year / 1–3 / 3–5 / 5+
- Typical driving context: urban / intercity / mixed / commercial

## Questions

1. What is the most recent road hazard or road-condition problem you remember encountering?
2. How did you discover it?
3. Did you already know about it before reaching the location?
4. Did you report it or warn someone? How?
5. Was the information trustworthy? Why or why not?
6. Did the condition change your route, timing, speed, or behavior?
7. Did it cause damage, delay, stress, or additional cost?
8. What do you currently use to learn about hazards on roads?
9. What is missing from those methods?
10. What evidence would make you trust a road-condition alert?
11. Would repeated observations from several independent trips make an alert more credible? Why?
12. What privacy concerns would you have with a phone collecting movement/location signals while driving?
13. For professional users: who receives road-condition reports today and how are they prioritized?
14. What would make a digital road-condition service useful enough to adopt?
15. What would make you reject it immediately?

## Post-interview coding

Record only summarized, anonymized findings.

- Pain severity: low / medium / high
- Current workaround: none / manual / app / calls / other
- Trust problem observed: yes / no
- Repeated-observation value: low / medium / high / unclear
- Privacy concern: low / medium / high
- Operational value: low / medium / high / unclear
- Quote-worthy insight: paraphrase only; no personal identifiers
