# G1.2 Decision Record — Competitive & Innovation Positioning

## Decision

**Continue RoadShield, but narrow the innovation claim.**

## What we will claim

RoadShield is an experimental road-condition intelligence platform that seeks to transform repeated, privacy-conscious observations from ordinary trips into corroborated, geospatially precise, explainable road-condition evidence.

## What we will not claim

- First smartphone pothole detector.
- First crowdsourced road-hazard platform.
- Replacement for Waze, Google Maps, or SNAR.
- Guaranteed accident prediction or prevention.
- Patentable invention without professional prior-art review.

## Why

Existing products and research already cover smartphone road sensing, pothole reporting, crowdsensing, and road-anomaly detection. The defensible opportunity is therefore a system-level evidence workflow that combines observation provenance, corroboration, temporal state, confidence, privacy constraints, and an operator-facing workflow.

## Gate impact

This decision narrows Phase 1 and changes the acceptance target for the field experiment:

> We are not trying to prove that a phone can detect a pothole. We are trying to determine whether repeated heterogeneous observations can be converted into a sufficiently reliable and explainable road-condition event for downstream action.

## Required proof

The field study must measure whether corroboration improves signal quality relative to a single observation baseline under varied phone placement, vehicle, and speed conditions.
