# RoadShield DZ — Official Sources & Product Boundaries

## Purpose
This document records authoritative external facts that constrain RoadShield's product positioning. It is not a substitute for legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Innovation-label requirements
Startup.dz's current Projet Innovant form requires, among other fields:
- founder identity and CV;
- project name and sector;
- a short project/innovation description limited to 400 characters;
- an innovation-focused Pitch Deck;
- a detailed project presentation and innovation aspects;
- a prototype photo;
- project progress level;
- business-model type (B2B/B2C/B2G/other);
- website;
- incubator information, if applicable;
- intellectual-property information, if applicable;
- competitions/rewards, if applicable.

Source: https://startup.dz/label-projet/

## 2. Competition eligibility
The 2026 President's Prize platform states that candidates must hold a valid official label (Projet Innovant, Startup or Scale-up). The platform currently states a 10 September 2026 deadline and DZD 3,000,000 for Best Innovative Project.

Source: https://startupoftheyear.dz/

## 3. Official Algerian road-safety data boundary
The Ministry of Interior describes SNAR as a national system for collecting and consolidating official bodily-injury road-accident data, improving data quality and transmission, spatial analysis, KPI generation, and data-mining-based accident analysis/prediction. The Ministry's road-code material also refers to national information systems for monitoring accidents and identifying black spots.

Source: https://interieur.gov.dz/le-systeme-national-des-accidents-de-la-route-snar/
Source: https://interieur.gov.dz/2026/01/07/le-ministre-de-linterieur-presente-au-conseil-de-la-nation-le-projet-de-texte-de-la-loi-portant-code-de-la-route/

## 4. Existing consumer hazard-reporting boundary
Waze already supports user reporting of road hazards, including potholes, objects and other hazards. Therefore RoadShield must not claim that simple hazard reporting is novel.

Source: https://support.google.com/waze/answer/13739290

## Product boundary derived from the evidence
RoadShield is not:
- a navigation replacement;
- a clone of Waze's manual hazard-reporting flow;
- a replacement for SNAR;
- an official accident-prediction system;
- an autonomous driving or vehicle-control system.

RoadShield's working thesis is narrower:
**field-sourced, sensor-backed road-condition evidence that is corroborated across trips, spatially aggregated, quality-scored, and converted into explainable road-risk intelligence for prevention and operational decisions.**

## Claims policy
Any statement about accuracy, cost savings, accident reduction, coverage, or market size must be labeled as measured evidence, an official fact, a hypothesis/target, or an estimate. Unsupported claims are prohibited.
