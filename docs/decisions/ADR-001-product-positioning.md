# ADR-001 — Product Positioning

## Status
Accepted for Gate 1

## Decision
RoadShield will be positioned as a **preventative road-safety intelligence layer**, not a navigation app and not a replacement for the official national accident-information system (SNAR).

## Why
Official SNAR is designed to collect/consolidate official crash data and support analysis of recorded bodily-injury accidents. RoadShield's thesis is to add field-sourced, sensor-backed observations from journeys and aggregate them into road-condition evidence.

## Consequences
Positive:
- clear product boundary;
- avoids direct competition with navigation as the primary use case;
- creates a defensible data/aggregation thesis;
- allows B2C, B2B and B2G pathways.

Negative:
- the product needs enough observations to become useful;
- field validation is mandatory;
- access to official road/infrastructure datasets, if desired later, requires lawful authorization and integration.

## Non-decision
This ADR does not claim that RoadShield is legally or commercially approved for government use. That is a later business/regulatory question.
